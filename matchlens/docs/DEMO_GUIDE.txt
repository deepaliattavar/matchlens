MatchLens — 110-second demonstration

PREPARE
Original 21-event sample, Riverside, Casual Fan, English. Local generation OFF. Start at checkpoint 4. Close personal notifications and tabs. Record the real app. Rehearse with a stopwatch; final video must be under two minutes.

0–15 seconds — Problem and visible contrast
“A recovery is only the start of the story. Here, one reaches a saved shot in eight seconds. Another possession ends without a shot. I built MatchLens to help a producer turn that difference into an explanation backed by event evidence.”
Show the two panels. Say “This is synthetic football data.”

15–35 seconds — Developing to observed
Click checkpoint 1, then checkpoint 2.
“At the recovery, the outcome stays open. When the shot arrives, the story updates and cites the recovery, pressure and shot events. Pressure is an association, not a proven cause.”
Point to evidence 3, 5, 7.

35–55 seconds — Audience and context
Switch to Analyst and open the sequence/evidence. Then switch to Español briefly.
“A casual fan gets a short explanation. An analyst can inspect the sequence and prior outcomes. Spanish fallback wording presents the same underlying facts.”
Do not call Spanish text AI-generated unless a real reviewed AI asset is shown.

55–80 seconds — Honest AI workflow
Return to Casual Fan and English; open Wording studio / Prepare or edit reusable wording.
“Microsoft Foundry Local runs Phi-4-mini on my laptop to draft wording. One draft confused a five-second pressure window with an exact interval, so I corrected and reviewed it. MatchLens reuses reviewed wording during replay. It does not wait for a new model response on every event.”
Show the saved shot wording. Do not click Generate. Original approval export is best evidence if available; reconstructed chat provenance is explicitly labelled.

80–100 seconds — Practical handoff
Return to Story desk; review the evidence, check the review box and download the producer package. Open an already-extracted example card if extracting would exceed the time budget.
“The producer can export the card together with its source events and review record. A changed story requires a new review.”
Static output only; no production video synchronization is claimed.

100–110 seconds — Close
“MatchLens combines an event pipeline, explainable analytics and reviewed AI wording around one question: they won the ball—what happened next?”

Do not add unlicensed music, logos, footage or club trademarks. If a screen interaction takes longer, trim narration or rerecord; do not accelerate a model-generation clip and present it as real-time performance. Verify final runtime and public playback.
